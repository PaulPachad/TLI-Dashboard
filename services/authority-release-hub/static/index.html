Why Moving Out of Google Apps Script Solves Your Friday Releases Permanently

Your writers and editors keep using the exact same 46 Google Sheets Upload Portals and Master Dashboard—nothing changes for your team. What changes is that the automation engine no longer runs inside a 6-minute-limited Google Sheet script.

Bottleneck Old Google Apps Script Bridge New FastAPI Release Hub (release.authoritymag.co)
Hard Execution Timeout Killed every 6 minutes; requires fragile 1-minute triggers & 9 KB state cursors that loop or stall. Up to 60 minutes continuous runtime in a single process—zero 1-minute triggers, zero lost cursors.
Scanning 46 Portals Opens 46 sheets one by one synchronously (~45–60 seconds every run). Reads all 46 portals concurrently via Google Sheets API in ~3 seconds.
3D Mockup Guard (Live + Follow-Up) "Decoupled Delivery" silently stripped missing mockups and sent plain-text emails. Hard MIME assertion: refuses to build Live OR Follow-Up email unless len(png_bytes) >= 10 KB.
Account / Quota Collisions Depends on which Google account is active in your browser tab when you click the menu. Uses dedicated GCP credentials for Cloud Run/GCS + permanent OAuth token for editor@authoritymag.co (1,500/day).
